We ran 1,000 prompt-injection attempts past a model that never writes a single token.

Jev (TypeSafe) returns a typed decision with a probability. No text. So we raced it against every current Claude model on the same 1,000 labelled texts, half attacks, half harmless, 25% French.

Jev → 93% correct, 0.5 s per decision, 3 cents per 1,000.

Claude Haiku 4.5 → 94% correct, 11 s per decision, $6 per 1,000 at list price.

Same accuracy. Twenty times faster. Two hundred times cheaper.

The interesting part is how they fail. Jev almost never flags a harmless message but misses about one attack in eight. Haiku catches nearly every attack but flags one harmless message in seven.
